Scope and exclusions

This page is intended as a practical errata for readers of the 2006 draft. Obvious spelling and grammar slips not individually listed by the author are not exhaustively catalogued. Two Takahashi misspellings explicitly described by Srednicki as publisher-introduced and absent from his original draft are excluded, as are published-book index corrections with no draft counterpart.

Reporting additional errors

Srednicki’s page asks readers who find further errors to contact him at the address printed on the draft. A public mirror of this errata should link to the draft on the author’s site rather than redistribute the PDF.
